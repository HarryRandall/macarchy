# Notes for AI agents

The rules for this repo are in **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)** and apply
to you exactly as they do to anyone else. Read it before changing anything.

Start with **[Docs/HANDOVER.md](Docs/HANDOVER.md)** for orientation, then
**[Docs/MIGRATION.md](Docs/MIGRATION.md)** for what is left to do.

Agent-specific only:

- Do not add `Co-Authored-By` trailers to commits.
- You cannot screenshot the app (the shell has no Screen Recording permission).
  To confirm a window rendered: `yabai -m query --windows | grep -i macarchy`.
- Verify claims against the machine rather than reporting what you expect. Say
  plainly when something is wrong, including when it is your own earlier work.
